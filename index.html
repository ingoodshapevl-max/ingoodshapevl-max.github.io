ASKEZA: Habits & Meditation

ASKEZA is a habit tracker and personal discipline application designed to help users build consistency, strengthen self-control and develop better daily routines.

Features include habit tracking, personal challenges, meditation, relaxation and nature sounds.

Шлях Йоги — Path of Yoga

Path of Yoga is an offline library of traditional yoga knowledge and practices. It brings together different yoga styles, asanas, pranayama, meditation, mantras and mudras.

The application also explores yoga philosophy, Vedic texts, Ayurveda, Jyotish and the traditional Panchang calendar.

Designed for people interested in studying and practising traditional yoga without requiring a constant internet connection.

Platform: Android

Get Path of Yoga on Google Play
